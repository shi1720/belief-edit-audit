This manuscript was prepared as a technical work sample for the January 2027
Iliad Intensive program application.

Iliad's /apply?direct page is a program application, not a conference paper
submission portal. The current form requests a CV and personal statements,
including a proposed alignment project. It has no manuscript-upload field.
The manuscript can therefore be linked as optional supporting material; it is
not submitted to a proceedings track through that form.

The Intensive's relevant themes are computational mechanics, belief-state
geometry, world models, causal interpretation, and safety guarantees and their
limits. Its published guidance permits applicants without a PhD; mathematical
maturity rather than a specific credential is the requirement. Selection and
immigration eligibility remain decisions of the program and relevant authorities.

Official sources inspected 17 October 2026:
https://www.iliad.ac/apply?direct
https://www.iliad.ac/intensive
https://iliad-intensive.org/

The public Intensive page advertises a US$5,000 travel-and-housing allowance.
The form has no separate grant-amount field. The application may request the
full advertised support in its additional-information field.

No conference acceptance, peer review, empirical neural-model improvement,
patentability, or guaranteed originality is represented by this work sample.
